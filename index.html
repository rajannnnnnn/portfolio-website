System Design · AI for Regulated Finance

ClauseCheck

RBI-grounded lending compliance auditor for Indian NBFCs

A fully specified AI system that audits NBFC loan files against Reserve Bank of India regulations — solving a problem most LLM tools get wrong: a citation is only correct if the regulation was actually in force on the date being judged. ClauseCheck ingests loan documents (Key Facts Statements, agreements, closure sets, collections-call transcripts), extracts roughly sixty typed fields per file, and returns a per-obligation verdict — compliant, violation, ambiguous, or not applicable — each backed by an exact clause citation scoped to the correct point in time.

The core design principle is enforced in code, not prompts: the system cannot cite a clause that wasn't in effect, and it declines to manufacture a violation where the rulebook is silent. Designed as a three-stage architecture with deterministic conflict detection and date-arithmetic checks layered on top of LLM-based extraction and retrieval.
